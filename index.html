About

I’m a junior at Stanford studying Political Science and Urban Studies on the pre-law track. I grew up in San Francisco, and a lot of what I’m interested in comes from thinking about how law and policy shape the places people live.

Interests

I’m especially interested in affordable housing, courts, democratic participation, and public interest law. I like questions about who has power, how institutions use it, and how people can influence the systems around them.

Project Interests

I’m Co-President of the Stanford Housing Equity Project and have also worked on affordable housing policy at Resources for Community Development in Berkeley. In this course, I’m interested in how AI might change politics, public institutions, and the way people engage with them.
